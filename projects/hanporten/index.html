HANporten.se — a practical guide to the meter’s local interface

An open guide to Swedish electricity meters’ HAN/P1 interfaces, with protocol information and examples for reading local data.

CONTEXT

The problem

Swedish electricity meters expose local customer interfaces with differing connectors and protocol conventions; builders need clear, usable guidance to connect their own equipment.

RESPONSIBILITY

What Henrik owned

Henrik is the main contributor to the public u9n repository behind HANporten.se, which is published by Utilitarian.

DELIVERY

Work delivered

  • Published guidance on the Swedish and Norwegian HAN/P1 variants
  • Documented connection details and meter-data interpretation
  • Made the documentation and examples openly available for community improvements
OUTCOME

Where it stands

A live public reference and open repository for HAN/P1 builders.
