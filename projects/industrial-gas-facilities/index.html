Utilitarian operations for gas DSO meter data collection

Ongoing product operations and support for Utilitarian in gas distribution meter-data collection.

CONTEXT

The problem

Utilitarian supports meter-data collection for gas DSOs, where product operations and device-specific metering knowledge both matter.

RESPONSIBILITY

What Henrik owned

Henrik supports and operates the Utilitarian product stack and provides gas DSO metering context and device support around the installed environments.

DELIVERY

Work delivered

  • Operated the Utilitarian web application and supporting services
  • Maintained PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ and Docker-based runtime components
  • Supported gas DSO meter-data collection workflows, device communication and field-related troubleshooting
  • Helped keep collection and operational data flows running across active DSO environments
APPROACH

Technical approach

The work is framed as ongoing product operations rather than a discrete consulting project: keep the platform healthy, understand the gas DSO metering context and resolve device or data-collection issues when they appear.

OUTCOME

Where it stands

Continuing operational support for Utilitarian-backed gas DSO meter-data collection across multiple environments.
